# Earth Vision — notes for Claude

## Next idea to pitch

The next time I ask for ideas (new features, improvements, "what could I add?"), pitch this one
first, in detail:

- **Let history play itself** ("laisser l'histoire se jouer"): pick a world and a number of
  years, and the countries live on their own — they expand, split, fall into civil war, form and
  break alliances, become vassals, lose provinces — following simple rules (size, neighbours,
  strength, terrain, rivers and crests as natural borders). Each step is recorded so it can be
  replayed, slowed down or stopped and edited by hand, and any moment can become a new world.
  A generator of alternate history.
